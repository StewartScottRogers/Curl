# Protocols

Every URL scheme Curl serves, one row per protocol library. There are two groups.

- **Original** protocols are curl's own. They behave like upstream curl 8.22.0 (the
  targeted version, `Gap/Baselines/target.json`, ADR-0479) and are matched against the
  platform's real curl: the Schannel build on Windows, the OpenSSL build on Linux and
  macOS. Their libraries are `Curl.Original.Protocol.<Name>.UnitLibrary`.
- **Extended** protocols are schemes Curl adds that upstream curl refuses (ADR-0999).
  They are tested against their own specifications, not against curl, and are put after
  curl's handlers, so a scheme both claim fails composition rather than replacing curl's
  handler. Setting the environment variable `CURL_EXTENSIONS=0` turns every one off
  (`ExtensionProtocolHandlers.For` in `Curl.Extension.Composition.UnitLibrary` then
  returns no handlers). Their libraries are `Curl.Extended.Protocol.<Name>.UnitLibrary`.

The schemes in each table are the ones the library's handler reports in its
`SupportedSchemes`, not a guess from the folder name. Both groups share the contracts in
`Curl.Protocol.Abstractions.UnitLibrary`. Option extensions (`Curl.Extension.*`), which
add no URL scheme of their own, are not listed here; `curl --extended-help` lists them.
